## How the list is made
Everything here comes from this browser: nothing is sent anywhere.

- **Mock papers.** When you finish a mock and type in your mark for each question, every mark you lost counts
  against the chapter that question revises. Only your latest marking of each mock counts, so redoing a mock
  replaces the old result.
- **Quizzes.** The last 10 answers you gave for each chapter's questions, in chapter quizzes and in
  [Practice quizzes](#practice.quiz). A wrong answer counts as much as 2 lost marks.
- **Checklists.** In a chapter you have marked as learned, each "I can" line you have not ticked counts as half a mark.

The bar shows how far behind each chapter is compared with the top one.

:::tip Use the list well
Work from the top. For each chapter: reread its key boxes, redo its worked examples with the answers covered,
then try the questions you lost marks on again. Don't spend all week on one chapter: two or three short sessions
on the top five beat one long session on the first.
:::
