## How this guide works

Every chapter is written for someone meeting the topic for the first time. It starts with the idea in plain English, builds up step by step, and finishes at real exam standard. Look out for these parts:

:::html
<div class="legend">
  <div><span class="sw" style="border-bottom:2px solid var(--red)">Aa</span><span><b>Key term cards</b> hold the exact definitions examiners expect. They are also collected in the <a href="#tools.glossary">glossary</a>.</span></div>
  <div><span class="sw" style="background:var(--card)"><span class="mk">M1</span></span><span><b>Worked examples</b> show every step, with marks: <b>M</b> for method, <b>A</b> for an accurate answer, <b>B</b> for an independent fact.</span></div>
  <div><span class="sw" style="background:var(--red-soft);color:var(--red)">✗</span><span><b>Common mistakes</b> are the errors examiners see every year, and how to avoid them.</span></div>
  <div><span class="sw" style="background:var(--hl-soft)">!</span><span><b>Exam technique</b> notes explain what the question really wants and where the marks are.</span></div>
  <div><span class="sw" style="border:1.5px dashed var(--ink-3)">≠</span><span><b>Board differences</b> say where Edexcel, AQA or OCR do things differently from Cambridge.</span></div>
  <div><span class="sw" style="background:var(--ok-soft);color:var(--ok)">✓</span><span><b>Try it yourself</b> questions have full answers, and <b>Quick checks</b> mark you instantly. Tick <b>Mark as learned</b> to track progress.</span></div>
</div>
:::
