## What this section is

The full courses in this guide (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and soon Biology and others) teach a whole syllabus. Many students take at least one subject this guide does not teach in full. For those, **Other subjects** will give a short guide to each, covering:

- what the subject is and what you actually do in it;
- how it is assessed (exams, essays, coursework, practical work), in general terms;
- the skills it builds and the kind of student it suits;
- which subjects it combines well with and which degrees or careers it supports;
- where to find the official specification and good free resources.

## Subjects planned

The section is planned in these groups:

- **Sciences and technology:** Information Technology, Environmental Management and Marine Science, and Physical Education.
- **English and languages:** English Literature, English Language, modern languages, and Classics and Latin.
- **Humanities and social sciences:** History, Geography, Sociology, Law, Politics and Government, Religious Studies and Philosophy, and Global Perspectives, Thinking Skills and the EPQ.
- **Creative subjects:** Art and Design, Music, Drama and Theatre, and Media and Film Studies.
- **More subjects at a glance:** short notes on further subjects.

## How to use it

Read the guides for every subject you are considering, then use [Choosing your subjects](#start.choose) to check that your combination keeps the doors open for the degrees and careers you are thinking about.
