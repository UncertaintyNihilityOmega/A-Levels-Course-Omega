# READER.md: what each chapter teaches (course order)

## bio.overview
Taught: the five CAIE 9700 papers (times, marks, what each holds), AS against A Level and the three routes, grades (a–e, A*–E), what is and is not printed in the exams (calculator yes, no formula sheet, Paper 1 no negative marking), assessment objectives AO1/AO2/AO3, the topic and chapter map, study order, the five habits of A* answers, command words (describe, explain, suggest) in outline.
Assumed: "stem" (of a question), "synoptic", "creditworthy", "degrees of freedom" and "critical values" (named only), the variable words independent, dependent, standardised and control (named only).

## bio.f.basics
Taught: the seven characteristics of living things; levels of organisation (molecule to ecosystem); animal and plant cell parts in one page; atom, element, molecule, compound, the six main elements, formulae; covalent bond, ion, ionic bond; polar molecule, water polarity, hydrogen bond, hydrophilic and hydrophobic, solute and solvent; acid, alkali, pH (logarithmic); catalyst, enzyme (name only); monomer, polymer, condensation, hydrolysis, the four groups of large molecule; photosynthesis and respiration equations, ATP (name); diffusion, concentration gradient, surface area to volume ratio; mm, µm, nm and the 1000 ladder, SI prefixes, size order, standard form, significant figures; percentage and percentage change, mean, anomalous result; reading graphs, gradient, tangent, initial rate, mean rate; command words state, describe, explain, suggest.
Assumed: the exam mark tokens [B1], [M1], [A1], [B0] and the ★ ratings (used from the first worked example, never explained); kinetic energy; chlorophyll, cell sap, cellulose (named in the cell table); osmosis and partially permeable (used in the potato example, taught later).

## bio.microscopy

Assumed: "photomicrograph" and "micrograph" (used without a definition); "vesicle" (practice question); "epidermis", "section" (thin section); "cilia" (SEM bullet); the meaning of "evaluate".

## bio.cells
Taught: eukaryotic cell, organelle, cell surface membrane (partially permeable), nucleus (envelope, pores, chromatin, nucleolus), ribosomes (80S and 70S, S value), rough and smooth ER, Golgi body, lysosome, secretory pathway and exocytosis, mitochondrion (cristae, matrix), chloroplast (grana, thylakoids, stroma), microtubule, centriole, cilium, microvillus, cell wall, plasmodesma, large permanent vacuole and tonoplast, plant against animal cell comparison, ATP as the energy currency, reading electron micrographs.

## bio.prokaryotes

## bio.carbs
Taught: monosaccharide, disaccharide, polysaccharide, macromolecule, (CH2O)n, triose, pentose, hexose; α and β glucose rings and how to draw them; glycosidic bond (1,4 and 1,6) by condensation, n units give n − 1 bonds; maltose, sucrose, lactose; reducing and non-reducing sugars and the acid-hydrolysis test; amylose, amylopectin, glycogen and why they suit storage; cellulose (β-glucose, straight chains, hydrogen bonds between chains, microfibrils); Mr calculations from condensation.

## bio.lipids

## bio.proteins
