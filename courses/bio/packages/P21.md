and the gold chapter first. Guide chapters (kit/STYLE.md "Guide chapters"): prose, tables, `:::tip`, `:::exam`,

## bio.exam.p1: Paper 1: multiple choice (`@paper CAIE 9700 Paper 1`, `@time 1–2 hours`)
Cover: the paper (40 four-option questions, 1 h 15 min, 40 marks, AS content, 31% of AS and 15.5% of A Level, one mark
each, no penalty for wrong answers so never leave a blank, soft pencil on the answer sheet, calculator allowed, rough
working on the question paper); about 1 min 50 s per question on average (75 ÷ 40: compute in the script); question
types with one original worked example each: recall, "which row is correct?" tables with ✓/✗, data and graph reading,
calculation (magnification, % change, SA:V), diagram identification (organelles drawn as SVG), sequence ordering,
"which statement is not correct"; techniques (cover the options and answer first; eliminate; check units; read every
word such as NOT and ONLY; mark and return); common traps by topic (a table of 10 misconceptions with the chapter to
revise, drawn from the AS package notes' pitfalls); a 10-question practice set in mock format (options as separate
paragraphs with bold letters, per COURSE.md) with answers and explanations; how to use real past papers (timed,
mark with the mark scheme, log every wrong answer by topic). `:::progress P1`.

## bio.exam.p24: Papers 2 and 4: structured questions (`@paper CAIE 9700 Papers 2 and 4`, `@time 2–3 hours`)
Cover: the two papers (Paper 2: AS, 1 h 15 min, 60 marks, 46% of AS and 23% of A Level; Paper 4: A Level content with
AS assumed, 2 h, 100 marks, 38.5% of A Level; answer on the paper; calculator; show working and units; marks in
brackets); marks-per-minute pacing (1.25 min per mark in Paper 2 and 1.2 min per mark in Paper 4: compute in the
script); the command-word table with the syllabus meanings (FACTS.md) and, for each, a model answer to a short
original question (define, state, describe, explain, suggest, compare, outline, discuss, calculate, predict, sketch,
identify, determine, assess, comment, contrast, give); how marking points work (one idea, one mark; precise terms;
"max" and alternatives; no credit for repeating the stem; contradictions cancel); describing graphs (trend, numbers
with units, turning points, rate changes) v explaining them; extended answers (plan in bullet points, one point per
mark, use the mark count); calculations (show working, units, sig figs; error carried forward); drawing and labelling
in the answer space; synoptic questions in Paper 4 (AS knowledge in A Level contexts: give two examples); a
"Spot the weak answer" `:::eg` for describe v explain; one full 8-mark Paper 2-style question and one 10-mark Paper
4-style question with mark schemes. `:::progress P2` and `:::progress P4`.

## bio.exam.p35: Papers 3 and 5: the practical skills papers (`@paper CAIE 9700 Papers 3 and 5`, `@time 2–3 hours`)
Cover: Paper 3 (2 h, 40 marks, two or three questions, laboratory practical: one investigation and microscope work;
half the candidates start with the microscope; ×10 eyepiece, ×10 and ×40 objectives; no dissection of animal
material; contexts may be unfamiliar and full instructions are given; the mark split by skill from FACTS.md); time
plan for Paper 3 (read everything first, start long timings early, draw while waiting); Paper 5 (1 h 15 min, 30 marks,
two or more questions, written, no laboratory; planning 14–16 marks, analysis, conclusions and evaluation 14–16;
may use AS and A Level content; unfamiliar contexts with information given); the skills checklists for both papers
condensed from FACTS.md, each linked to bio.practical, bio.practical2, bio.stats, bio.planning or bio.evaluation;
the most common lost marks (a table: units in table bodies, fewer than five IV values, vague standardised variables,
shading in drawings, joining points wrongly, "human error", conclusions beyond the data); how to practise Paper 3
without a lab (use past-paper results, practise drawing from real slides or images, time yourself); one worked
Paper 5 planning example (8 marks) and one Paper 3 table-and-graph example. `:::board` box allowed here: the AQA
practical difference in COURSE.md only. `:::progress P3` and `:::progress P5`.
