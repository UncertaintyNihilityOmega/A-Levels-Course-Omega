## bio.mock.p3: Mock Paper 3 (`@time 2 h`, `@paper CAIE 9700 Paper 3`)
Paper 3 is a hands-on laboratory exam. This mock is a **written version with supplied readings and drawings**: say so
in the `:::exam` box ("In the real exam you carry out the procedures yourself; here the results and specimens are
supplied so you can practise every other skill").
- **Total 40 marks, 2 questions** (June 2024 had 21 + 19: FACTS.md). Question 1 (about 21–23 marks): an investigation
  in an AS context (e.g. effect of a solute concentration on a plant tissue, or a semi-quantitative Benedict's or
  enzyme reaction with colour standards). Give the method the student "carried out" in numbered steps, then supplied
  raw readings (including one anomaly) and qualitative observations. Ask for: identify variables; decide intervals
  and a dilution table; assess a hazard as low, medium or high; record results in a table they construct (the
  answer shows the model table); calculate means or rates with correct sig figs; describe the graph they would plot
  (axes, scale, points, line) and use a supplied graph to read an unknown; conclusions; sources of error (systematic v
  random) and improvements; an extension.
- Question 2 (about 17–19 marks): microscope and photomicrograph work, with SVG drawings standing in for a slide and
  a photomicrograph (e.g. a plant stem or leaf TS, or a blood smear): plan diagram task (the answer lists the marking
  points: no cells, correct proportions, layers shown, clear lines, labels); a high-power cell drawing task; an
  eyepiece graticule calibration and actual size calculation; a scale-bar calculation; a comparison table of two
  specimens (observable features only).
- Marks per question and the skills split should resemble FACTS.md (manipulation, measurement and observation 15–17;
  presentation 11–13; analysis 11–13). The check script asserts the total is 40.

## bio.mock.p5: Mock Paper 5 (`@time 1 h 15 min`, `@paper CAIE 9700 Paper 5`)
- **Total 30 marks, 2 questions** (June 2024 had 19 + 11: FACTS.md). `:::exam` instructions: answer all questions;
  calculator; show working and units.
- Question 1 (about 17–19 marks): an unfamiliar context with background information; ask for a hypothesis and a
  sketch-graph prediction, the IV, DV and standardised variables, a full method (including concentrations in % (w/v) or
  mol dm⁻³ and a dilution), controls, replicates, risk assessment, and how to assess the reliability of results
  (SD or SE); then a short data-analysis part.
- Question 2 (about 11–13 marks): supplied data (a table with means, SD or SE, or a graph with error bars) in an
  unfamiliar context: processing (means, % change), choosing and justifying a statistical test, a null hypothesis,
  finishing a partly completed calculation with the formula and critical value given, conclusions, the extent the
  hypothesis is supported, and evaluation.
- Planning and analysis marks each 14–16 overall (FACTS.md). Check script asserts the total is 30 and recomputes all
  numbers (import data/crit.py).
