# P23: A Level mixed practice and Mock Paper 4

Chapters: `bio.mixed.a2` (`@level guide`), `bio.mock.p4` (`@level mock`). No widgets. Read COURSE.md, kit/STYLE.md
(especially "Mock papers") and the gold chapter first. Original questions only, in the board's style; every question
has a `(#chapter-id)` revise link; check scripts recompute every number and the paper total.

## bio.mixed.a2: Mixed practice: A Level (`@time 5–7 hours`)
Mixed questions across topics 12–19 with AS content used synoptically, ★ to ★★★, interleaved. Sections: (1) 15 quick
recall and calculation questions (RQ, Rf, χ², Hardy–Weinberg, Lincoln index, Simpson's D, PCR copies, conduction
speed, sarcomere band lengths); (2) 8 data-response questions with tables or `:::plot` graphs; (3) 6 extended synoptic
questions (e.g. chemiosmosis in mitochondria and chloroplasts; ADH and water potential; insulin from gene to
receptor; natural selection and antibiotic resistance; speciation and meiosis; gene therapy and the immune response);
(4) statistics set: one each of χ², t-test, Spearman, Pearson, SE and 95% CI, with critical values given in the stem.
`:::progress P4` and `:::progress P5` at the end.

## bio.mock.p4: Mock Paper 4 (`@time 2 h`, `@paper CAIE 9700 Paper 4`)
- **Total 100 marks**, 10 questions (as in June 2024: FACTS.md), each 8–15 marks, with parts and subparts; totals such
  as 8, 9, 11, 11, 10, 11, 9, 15, 8, 8 (choose your own split summing to 100).
- `:::exam` instructions: answer all questions; calculator allowed; show all working and use appropriate units; marks in
  brackets.
- Content: topics 12–19 in roughly syllabus order, AS knowledge required in places. Include: one statistics calculation
  with the formula printed in the question and the critical-value table given (degrees of freedom not given), one
  genetics cross with a genetic diagram, one data question on respiration or photosynthesis, one nervous or muscle
  question with a diagram, one homeostasis question, one genetic technology question with a gel or PCR, one ecology or
  conservation question, and at least one extended answer of 5–7 marks ("explain" or "discuss").
- Mark scheme `A:` in exam wording, one marking point per token, alternatives and "also credit" lines in brackets,
  ecf noted for calculations. New contexts and data, not reused from chapters.
