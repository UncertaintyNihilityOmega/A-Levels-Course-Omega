# P22: AS mixed practice and AS mocks

Chapters: `bio.mixed.as` (`@level guide`), `bio.mock.p1`, `bio.mock.p2` (`@level mock`). No widgets. Read COURSE.md,
kit/STYLE.md (especially "Mock papers") and the gold chapter first. All questions are original, in the board's style
and mark-scheme language (never copy past papers). Every question's `(#chapter-id)` points to the chapter to revise.
Check scripts recompute every number (and for the mocks, that the question marks add up to the paper total).

## bio.mixed.as: Mixed practice: AS (`@time 4–6 hours`)
Mixed questions across topics 1–11, ordered from ★ to ★★★, deliberately interleaved (no two neighbours from the same
topic). Sections: (1) 15 quick recall and calculation questions; (2) 8 data-response questions with tables or
`:::plot` graphs (describe and explain); (3) 6 extended synoptic questions joining two or more topics (e.g. enzymes and
membranes in digestion and absorption; DNA replication and mitosis; haemoglobin structure and oxygen transport;
water potential and transport in plants; antibodies and protein structure; cholera and osmosis); (4) 10 multiple-choice
questions in quiz form. Each answer has marks and a revise link. Add a `:::progress P1` and `:::progress P2` at the end.

## bio.mock.p1: Mock Paper 1 (`@time 1 h 15 min`, `@paper CAIE 9700 Paper 1`)
- Exactly **40 questions, 1 mark each, total 40**, four options A–D, one correct. `:::exam` instructions box copying the
  real rubric's meaning (FACTS.md "What the papers print"): answer all forty questions, four possible answers, choose
  one, each correct answer scores one mark, calculator allowed.
- One `:::try` per question, `Q: [1 mark] (#chapter) …`, options as separate paragraphs with a blank line between and
  a bold letter (`**A** …`), `A:` the letter plus a one-line reason (`**C** [B1] …`).
- Coverage in syllabus order like the real paper: topics 1–11 roughly in proportion (about 3–5 questions per topic;
  cell structure and molecules a little more). Mix: about one third recall, two thirds application and data (tables,
  graphs described in the stem or drawn as small SVGs, calculations such as magnification, % change, SA:V, ratios).
  At least 6 "which row is correct" tables (Markdown table with columns 1, 2, 3 and rows A–D using ✓ and ✗).
- Distractors are real misconceptions from the package notes' pitfalls. Spread the correct letters evenly (about 10
  each) and check that in the script.

## bio.mock.p2: Mock Paper 2 (`@time 1 h 15 min`, `@paper CAIE 9700 Paper 2`)
- **Total 60 marks**, 6 questions (as in June 2024: FACTS.md), each 5–17 marks with parts (a), (b), (c) … and roman
  subparts, mark totals per question like 14, 12, 6, 13, 9, 6 (choose your own split summing to 60).
- `:::exam` instructions: answer all questions; calculator allowed; show all working and use appropriate units; marks
  in brackets.
- Content: AS topics only, each question centred on one topic but with synoptic parts; at least one diagram to label
  (SVG), one calculation (magnification or % change), one graph or table to describe and explain, one 4–5 mark
  extended explanation, one genetic code or DNA question, one practical-context part (e.g. a food test or osmosis
  investigation). Mark scheme `A:` lines in exam wording, one marking point per token, alternatives in brackets.
- Use contexts and data not used in any chapter's worked examples.
