Chapters: **cs.mock.p1**, **cs.mock.p2** (`@level mock`). No widget. Write after P19 exists. Read COURSE.md,
kit/STYLE.md ("Mock papers"), chapters/cs.datatypes.md (gold) and FACTS.md (the papers, what each paper prints and

paper. Each mock needs a checks file (numbers, traces, query results; runs any Python).

## Format rules for both
- `@time` is the real time; the `:::exam` box holds the real front-page instructions (FACTS.md): answer all questions,
  no calculator, no marks for brand names, marks in brackets, total 75.
- One bare `:::try` per question, `Q: [n marks] (#chapter-to-revise) …`, parts (a), (b) … with a blank line before
  each; `A:` is the full mark scheme: one `[B1]` per mark point, alternatives and "accept/reject" notes in round
  brackets; model pseudocode in fences.
- Total exactly **75** (`check` adds the marks).
- `@summary` says what the paper covers; `Next:` to the next chapter in course order.

## cs.mock.p1 · Mock Paper 1: Theory Fundamentals
`@time 1 h 30 min`. About 8–9 questions of 4–14 marks (the May/June 2025 paper had 8, 4 to 14 marks), every one a
short scenario, covering all of sections 1–8: data representation (conversion, two's complement arithmetic, BCD/hex
use), multimedia and compression (a file-size estimate without a calculator, RLE), networks/internet (topology or
IP/DNS), hardware (a device's operation, RAM/ROM), logic (expression from a circuit drawn as hand SVG, truth table
with working), processor and assembly (register transfer; a trace with the instruction-set table printed in the
question, as the real paper does), system software, security and integrity (a parity block or check digit), ethics or
licences, databases (normalisation, SQL query with a two-table join). Balance AO1 (recall) about 60% and AO2
(application) about 40% (FACTS.md).

`@time 2 h`. **7 questions** (as the May/June 2025 paper), 75 marks, sections 9–12, answers in pseudocode. The real
