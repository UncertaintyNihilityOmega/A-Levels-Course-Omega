## chem.exam.p1 · Paper 1 survival guide: multiple choice (`@level guide`, `@time 1–2 hours`)
`@paper CAIE 9701 Paper 1`. Prose-led (tables, `:::tip`, `:::exam`, `:::warn`, `:::recap`); this guide may include one
`:::try` of 8 worked MCQs with full reasoning (the check accepts `:::try` in guides) and a `:::quiz` of 6 more.
Sections: the paper (FACTS.md: 1 h 15 min, 40 four-choice questions, 40 marks, AS content, 31% of AS / 15.5% of A Level,
AO1 and AO2 equally); timing (under 2 minutes per question; a first pass answering the sure ones, a second pass for
calculations); question types you will meet (recall; calculation; data or graph reading; "which statements are correct"
items written as four choices such as "1, 2 and 3 / 1 and 2 only / 2 and 3 only / 1 only" — describe the format only
as "some questions"); techniques (cover the options, eliminate, estimate calculations, check units and powers of ten,
watch "not" and "incorrect", draw a quick structure for organic questions); traps by topic, each linked to its chapter
(moles: cm³ vs dm³; bonding: shapes with lone pairs; energetics: sign of ΔH; equilibria: catalyst and K; kinetics:
Boltzmann curve at a higher T; organic: counting isomers, reagents vs conditions); the Data booklet (what is in it:
FACTS.md list; learn where things are); practice plan (topic quizzes in each chapter, then [Mock paper: Paper 1 multiple choice](#chem.mock.p1),
then the board's real past papers: through a school, the Cambridge website or the PMT topic collection in COURSE.md).
Worked MCQs: original, AS content, each with the full reasoning and why each wrong option is wrong. A `:::progress P1`
block. `:::recap`. `Next: [Papers 2 and 4 survival guide: structured questions](#chem.exam.p24)`.

## chem.mock.p1 · Mock paper: Paper 1 multiple choice (`@level mock`)
`@time 1 h 15 min`, `@paper CAIE 9701 Paper 1`, total **40 marks**: exactly 40 questions, each 1 mark.
- An `:::exam` box first: 40 questions, four options each (A, B, C, D), one correct answer, 1 mark each, Data booklet
  allowed, no penalty for wrong answers (state this only as "a wrong answer scores zero").
- **One bare `:::try` per question**: `Q: [1 mark] (#chapter-id) question text` followed by the four options on separate
  lines ("A …", "B …", "C …", "D …"; a blank line before A as for parts), and `A: **C**. one or two lines of reasoning,
  with why the most tempting wrong option is wrong`. Calculations: the check script recomputes the correct option and
  every numerical distractor.
- Spread (this mock's design, not a claim about real papers): about 15 questions on AS physical chemistry (topics 1–8),
  9 on inorganic (9–12), 16 on organic and analysis (13–22); questions in syllabus-topic order like a real paper;
  difficulty rising within each block; at least 12 calculations, 4 "which statements are correct" items, 3 data/graph
  items, and 2 that need the Data booklet (bond energies, IE values).
- Distractors are real misconceptions (wrong ratio, cm³ vs dm³, the conjugate pair confusion, Markovnikov minor product,
  SN1 vs SN2, wrong sign of ΔH).
- No figure is required; if a question needs a structure, use a condensed formula or a small skeletal SVG.
