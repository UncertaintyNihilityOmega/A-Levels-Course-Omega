## chem.mixed.as · Mixed practice: AS Level (`@level guide`, `@time 6–8 hours`)
`@paper CAIE 9701 Papers 1 and 2`. Purpose: interleaved practice across all AS topics (1–22), after the topic chapters.
- A short opening (why mixed practice works: retrieving from memory across topics; how to use the page: one set per
  sitting, mark yourself with the hints closed) and a `:::tip` on using the revise links.
- **Six sets**, each a `:::try Set n: …` with 8 questions mixing at least five topics, from ★ to ★★★, each
  `Q: [n marks] (#chapter-id) …` with `H:` hints on the ★★★ ones and `A:` answers marked line by line ([B1], [M1], [A1]).
  Set themes: 1 foundations (atoms, moles, bonding); 2 energy and equilibria; 3 kinetics, acids and redox;
  4 inorganic (Period 3, Group 2, Group 17, nitrogen); 5 organic reactions and mechanisms; 6 synthesis, analysis and
  synoptic problems (e.g. a mass-spectrum plus IR plus moles question; a Group 2 thermal decomposition with gas volume).
- Then a `:::quiz` of 10 MCQs mixing topics, in Paper 1 style.
- A `:::checklist` with one line per AS group ("I can answer mixed questions on…"), a `:::recap` with the five most
  common mistakes seen across the sets, and `Next: [Mixed practice: A Level](#chem.mixed.a2)`.
- checks/chem.mixed.as.py recomputes every number. Data values only from COURSE.md/FACTS.md.

## chem.mock.p2 · Mock paper: Paper 2 AS structured questions (`@level mock`)
`@time 1 h 15 min`, `@paper CAIE 9701 Paper 2`, total **60 marks** exactly.
- `:::exam` box: time, total marks, answer all questions, Data booklet allowed, show working, appropriate s.f.
- Five structured questions (one bare `:::try` each, parts (a), (b), (c)… with a blank line before each part,
  `Q: [n marks] (#chapter)` per question, full mark scheme in `A:`), for example:
  1. [11] Atomic structure and moles: isotopes and Ar from data, a configuration, successive IE deduction, and an
     empirical-formula calculation (#chem.atoms).
  2. [13] Energetics and equilibria: a calorimetry calculation, a Hess cycle, a Kc calculation and a Le Chatelier
     explanation (#chem.energetics).
  3. [12] Inorganic: Period 3 oxides/chlorides with water and pH, a Group 2 decomposition with a gas-volume calculation,
     a halide test (#chem.periodicity).
  4. [12] Organic: a reaction scheme from an alkene through a halogenoalkane and alcohol to a carbonyl, reagents and
     conditions, one mechanism with curly arrows described, isomers (#chem.org.hydrocarbons).
  5. [12] Analysis and synthesis: IR and mass spectrum interpretation, [M+1] carbon count, a two-step synthesis
     (#chem.analysis).
  Marks 11 + 13 + 12 + 12 + 12 = 60. Show each part's marks in the question text as "(3 marks)" in round brackets (never
  bare "[3]", which the page could read as a mark token); the mark tokens go in the answer lines.
- checks/chem.mock.p2.py recomputes every number.
