## phys.f.basics: Physics from zero
`@level pre` · (no `@paper` for pre) · `@time 4–6 hours` · `Next: [Physical quantities, units and uncertainty](#phys.units)`
For readers with weak or no IGCSE physics, or who need a refresher. A `pre` chapter still has the full checklist
(`:::why`, `:::def`, 3+ `:::eg`, 5+ `:::try`, 3+ `:::quiz`, `:::recap`, `checks/phys.f.basics.py`), but at a gentle
level, with many small examples. Link generously to the maths foundations:
[Numbers, fractions and percentages](#math.f.numbers), [Algebra from scratch](#math.f.algebra),
[Indices, surds and standard form](#math.f.powers), [Graphs and straight lines](#math.f.graphs),
[Geometry and trigonometry basics](#math.f.geometry).

### What to cover (IGCSE-level content the 9702 syllabus assumes, plus the maths skills physics uses)
1. The physicist's toolkit: standard form and significant figures (with physics numbers: 3.00 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹,
   1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ C), rounding to the data's s.f.; units and the most common prefixes (k, M, m, μ, n).
2. Rearranging formulas (v = s/t, P = VI, ρ = m/V, E = ½mv², v² = u² + 2as): the method (do the same to both sides;
   undo operations in reverse order), with five graded examples.
3. Graphs: straight lines y = mx + c; gradient and intercept with units; direct proportion (straight line through the
   origin) vs inverse proportion (y ∝ 1/x); reading the area under a graph (speed–time → distance).
4. Trigonometry for physics: sin, cos, tan in a right-angled triangle; Pythagoras; components of a force at an angle
   (preview of [Physical quantities, units and uncertainty](#phys.units)).
5. Areas and volumes physics uses: circle (area of a wire's cross-section from its diameter), sphere (surface area 4πr²,
   volume), cylinder.
6. IGCSE physics in one page each (short sections, one `:::def` and one example each): speed and acceleration; mass,
   weight, forces and resultant force; energy stores and transfers, work and power; pressure and density; current,
   voltage and resistance in a simple circuit; waves (wavelength, frequency, speed; the EM spectrum in order);
   the atom (protons, neutrons, electrons) and radioactivity (α, β, γ). Each ends with a link to the A Level
   chapter that develops it.

### Definitions (`:::def`, IGCSE-level but correct)
Speed, acceleration, force, weight, energy, power, density, pressure, current, potential difference, resistance,
wavelength, frequency (keep them consistent with the wording used in the A Level packages: e.g. acceleration "rate
of change of velocity").

### Worked examples and question ideas
Standard form conversions; rearranging; a gradient with units from a graph; a resolved force; a wire's
cross-sectional area from 0.50 mm diameter (1.96 × 10⁻⁷ m²); a density; a circuit with V = IR; a wave speed.
`:::try` 8–10 short questions; `:::quiz` 5–6 questions with misconceptions (mass vs weight, current used up, etc.).
No "Exam practice ★ to ★★★" section is needed for a `pre` chapter: instead a `:::try Ready for AS?` set of 5 questions
that preview AS style. A short `:::checklist` and `:::recap`.

### Figures
A straight-line graph with gradient triangle (`:::plot`); a right-angled triangle with a force and its components
(hand SVG); a simple series circuit with an ammeter and voltmeter (copy the gold chapter's symbols).
