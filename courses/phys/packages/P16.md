# P16: Mixed practice

Chapters, in this order: **phys.mixed.as**, **phys.mixed.a2**. No widget to build.
Read first: COURSE.md, kit/STYLE.md, chapters/phys.dc.md (the gold chapter), then this file. Nothing else.
These are practice chapters (`@level ref` in course.json and in the file header). They come after every topic chapter,
so they draw questions from all of them. They are not mock papers (those come later): no timer, no fixed paper total.

### Shape of each chapter
```
@chapter phys.mixed.as
@title Mixed practice: AS Level
@level ref
@paper CAIE P1 · P2 (AS)
@time 6–8 hours
@summary …
```
1. A short introduction (2–3 sentences) and a `:::tip` on how to use the page (do a set under timed conditions:
   about 1.25 minutes per mark for Paper 2-style and 1.2 for Paper 4-style questions; mark yourself honestly
   with the scheme; revise the linked chapter for every lost mark).
2. `:::quiz Paper 1-style multiple choice` (AS page only): 15 original four-option questions across topics 1–11,
   each wrong option a real misconception, each `>` explanation naming the chapter.
3. Several `:::try` sets, each titled by a theme (e.g. `:::try Set A: mechanics and materials`,
   `:::try Set B: waves and electricity`, `:::try Set C: synoptic`), 4–6 questions per set, about 14–20 questions in
   total. Every question: `Q: [n marks] (#chapter-id) ★★ …` with multi-part (a), (b), (c) structure like real
   structured questions, 2–3 `H:` hints on ★★★ questions, and an `A:` that is a full mark scheme with C1/A1/B1/M1
   tokens at the end of the lines that earn them (the marks must add up to [n marks]: `check` enforces it).
4. At least a third of the questions must be **synoptic** (crossing two or more chapters), because Paper 4 assumes AS
   knowledge and Paper 2 mixes topics.
5. `:::progress P1` and `:::progress P2` on the AS page (`:::progress P4` on the A Level page) so readers can see their
   checklist state, then `:::recap` with a short "what to revise next" list linking chapters, and the `Next:` line.
6. `checks/<id>.py` recomputes every number. Use data-sheet constants (COURSE.md).

### phys.mixed.as: Mixed practice: AS Level
`Next: [Mixed practice: A Level](#phys.mixed.a2)`. Topics 1–11 (chapters phys.units … phys.particles, including
phys.dc). Cover every AS chapter at least once. Ideas: a car braking and the energy dissipated (kinematics +
energy); a projectile launched by a spring (solids + kinematics); a stationary wave on a wire whose tension and
Young modulus are linked (superposition + solids); a heater circuit with internal resistance and resistivity
(electricity + d.c.); a collision with momentum and KE and a v–t graph (dynamics + kinematics); a floating object
and pressure (forces); α-decay recoil (particles + momentum); a CRO measuring the frequency of a sound and a Doppler
shift (waves); uncertainty in a derived quantity (units).

### phys.mixed.a2: Mixed practice: A Level
`Next:` none of the topic chapters: end with `Next: [Mock paper: Paper 1 multiple choice](#phys.mock.p1)` (the mocks follow).
Topics 12–25 plus AS synoptic content. Cover every A Level chapter at least once. Ideas: a satellite changing orbit
(circular + gravity + energy); a charged particle accelerated by a p.d., then entering a magnetic field (efields +
magnetic + circular); a capacitor discharging through a resistor heating water (capacitance + temperature); a
mass–spring system with damping and resonance (oscillations); a gas heated at constant pressure with the first law
(gases + thermo); photoelectric emission and the stopping p.d. (quantum + efields); a radioactive tracer for PET with
activity and photon energies (nuclear + medical); a star's radius and distance (astro); a generator's r.m.s. output
(induction + ac); a Hall probe measurement (magnetic); the energy released by a fission and the number of decays per
second needed for a given power (nuclear + energy).
