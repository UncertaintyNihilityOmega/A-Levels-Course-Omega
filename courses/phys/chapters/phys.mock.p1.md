Q: [1 mark] (#phys.units) The resistance \(R\) of a wire of length \(L\) and cross-sectional area \(A\) is given by \(R = \rho L/A\), where \(\rho\) is the resistivity of the material. What are the SI base units of \(\rho\)?

**A** \(\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)

**B** \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)

**C** \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{3}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)

**D** \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{5}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)
A: **C**. \(R = V/I\) has base units \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{2}\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\), and \(\rho = RA/L\) multiplies these by \(\mathrm{m^2/m} = \mathrm{m}\). [B1]

Q: [1 mark] (#phys.units) The kinetic energy of a trolley is calculated from \(E = \tfrac12 mv^2\). Its mass is \(0.250 \pm 0.005\,\mathrm{kg}\) and its speed is \(3.20 \pm 0.04\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\). What is the percentage uncertainty in \(E\)?

**A** \(2.6\%\)

**B** \(3.3\%\)

**C** \(4.5\%\)

**D** \(6.5\%\)
A: **C**. \(\dfrac{0.005}{0.250} = 2.0\%\) and \(\dfrac{0.04}{3.20} = 1.25\%\); the power of 2 doubles the second, so \(2.0 + 2 \times 1.25 = 4.5\%\) (**B** forgets the square). [B1]

**A** X and Y hit the ground at the same time.
