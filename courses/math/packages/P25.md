## math.fm.complex · De Moivre's theorem and trigonometry (6–8 hours)
Outcomes (FACTS.md 2.5a–c, first three uses):
- 2.5a understand de Moivre's theorem, for a positive or negative integer exponent, in terms of the geometrical effect
  of multiplication and division of complex numbers;
- 2.5b prove de Moivre's theorem for a positive integer exponent (e.g. by induction);
- 2.5c use de Moivre's theorem for a positive or negative rational exponent: to express trigonometrical ratios of
  multiple angles in terms of powers of trigonometrical ratios of the fundamental angle (e.g. cos 5θ in terms of
  cos θ, tan 5θ in terms of tan θ); to express powers of sin θ and cos θ in terms of multiple angles (e.g. sin⁶θ in
  terms of cos 2θ, cos 4θ and cos 6θ).
Cover: (cos θ + i sin θ)ⁿ = cos nθ + i sin nθ, the picture (n rotations by θ, modulus raised to n); proof by
induction for positive n as an `:::eg (Exam style)` with induction marks (FURTHER.md pattern), extension to negative n
(1/z); e^(iθ) form; powers of complex numbers in polar form; multiple angles: binomial expansion of (c + is)ⁿ, equate
real and imaginary parts, use s² = 1 − c²; tan nθ as a quotient divided through by cosⁿθ; powers: z + 1/z = 2 cos θ,
z − 1/z = 2i sin θ, zⁿ + z⁻ⁿ = 2 cos nθ, expand (z ± 1/z)ⁿ and pair terms (June 2025 FP2 Q3 shape [6]); integrals
of sin⁴θ, cos⁵θ via these; solving trig equations using a multiple-angle identity (cos 3θ cubic roots give exact
values such as cos(π/9)).
Pitfalls: i² sign errors in binomial expansions; dropping the 2ⁿ factor; pairing terms wrongly.

## math.fm.complex2 · Roots of unity and complex series (6–7 hours)
Outcomes (FACTS.md 2.5c, last two uses): use de Moivre's theorem in the summation of series (e.g. the "C + iS"
method to sum series such as Σ ⁿCᵣ sin rθ) and in finding and using the nth roots of unity.
Cover: zⁿ = 1 roots e^(2πik/n), a regular polygon on the unit circle (a figure), their sum is 0 (geometric series
or symmetry), 1 + ω + ω² = 0 for cube roots; roots of zⁿ = w for any w (modulus root, argument (θ + 2πk)/n, the
range −π < θ ≤ π, June 2025 FP2 Q1 shape [5]); using roots to factorise zⁿ − 1 into real quadratics; roots of
(z + 1)ⁿ = zⁿ-type equations; geometric uses (vertices of a polygon centred at a point); C + iS: form the complex
series, sum it as a GP or a binomial expansion, take real/imaginary parts (Σ cos rθ, Σ ⁿCᵣ cos rθ = 2ⁿcosⁿ(θ/2)
cos(nθ/2)), the half-angle trick e^(iθ) − 1 = e^(iθ/2)·2i sin(θ/2).
Pitfalls: arguments outside the required range; forgetting one root; dividing the modulus's root wrongly.
Widget: embed **fm-demoivre** in both chapters (powers in complex, roots in complex2).

### Widget spec: fm-demoivre (widgets/fm-demoivre.js)
- `data-mode="powers|roots"`, `data-z="r,theta"` (modulus and argument in radians, default "1.1,0.5"), `data-n="5"`.
- powers: Argand canvas with z, z², …, zⁿ as points joined in order (a spiral when r ≠ 1), each labelled; slider for
  n (−6…12, negative powers too) and number inputs for r, θ; readout |zⁿ| = rⁿ, arg zⁿ = nθ reduced to (−π, π].
- roots: the n roots of w = r e^(iθ) as a regular polygon, labelled z₀ … zₙ₋₁, with the circle of radius r^(1/n);
  readouts list each root as R e^(iφ) with φ in (−π, π] and in a + bi form to 3 s.f.; "sum of roots = 0" readout.
- Keyboard: sliders and inputs only; W.axes with equal scales; ranges follow the points. `.w-err` on bad input.
  Node-test the root list against Python's `cmath`.
