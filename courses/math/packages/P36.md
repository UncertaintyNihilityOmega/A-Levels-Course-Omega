## The real papers (FACTS.md "Real papers, June 2025")
- Paper 3 (FM): `@time 1 h 30 min`, total **50**, the real paper had 7 questions (5 to 10 marks); syllabus 5–7. Its
  front page adds "Where a numerical value for the acceleration due to gravity (g) is needed, use 10 m s–2": copy that
  rule into the `:::exam` box. Mock: 6 or 7 questions, total exactly 50, covering projectiles (trajectory), centres
  of mass with toppling or equilibrium, elastic strings with energy, horizontal and vertical circles, variable force
  (v dv/dx), direct and oblique impact; the last two long (9–10 marks).
- Paper 4 (FS): `@time 1 h 30 min`, total **50**, the real paper had 6 questions (6 to 12 marks); syllabus 5–7. Mock: 6
  questions, total exactly 50, covering a piecewise PDF with CDF of a related variable, a t-test or interval
  (one-sample or pooled), a normal two-sample test, a χ² goodness of fit or contingency test, a Wilcoxon test with a
  sign-test comparison, and a PGF. Data as the real paper gives it (summaries Σx, Σ(x − x̄)², tables); critical values
  from the MF19 tables, recomputed in the check (FURTHER.md helpers in packages/P31.md and P32.md).
- Both: `:::exam` instructions as on the real front page; one bare `:::try` per question with the full mark scheme;
  a table "Question → chapter to revise". The check asserts the total (50) and recomputes every answer.
