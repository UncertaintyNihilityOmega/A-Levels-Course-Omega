## The real papers (FACTS.md "Real papers, June 2025")
- Paper 1 (FP1): `@time 2 h`, total **75**, the real paper had 7 questions (8 to 16 marks each); the syllabus says
  6–8 structured questions. Mock: 7 or 8 questions, total exactly 75, every FP1 topic (roots, series, matrices and
  transformations, induction, rational functions and related graphs, polar, vectors and planes), rising in
  difficulty; the last two are long (13–16 marks) and synoptic (e.g. a rational function with sketches and an
  inequality; planes with a skew-line distance and an unknown).
- Paper 2 (FP2): `@time 2 h`, total **75**, the real paper had 8 questions (5 to 15 marks); syllabus 7–9. Mock: 8
  questions, total 75, covering hyperbolic functions, matrices (systems, eigenvectors, Cayley–Hamilton), calculus
  (differentiation, Maclaurin, integration, reduction formulae, rectangles, arc length or surface area), complex
  numbers (de Moivre, roots) and DEs (one first order, one second order, each around 10 marks).
- Both: `:::exam` instructions as on the real front page (answer all questions; MF19 provided; calculator where
  appropriate, no marks for unsupported calculator answers; 3 s.f., angles 1 d.p.); then one bare `:::try` per
  question with the full mark scheme (M1, A1, B1, A1 FT, DM1 as in FURTHER.md, accepted alternatives); a table
  "Question → chapter to revise". The check asserts the total (75) and recomputes every answer.
- Proof questions (induction in Paper 1, de Moivre or a "prove" identity or a reduction formula in Paper 2) are
  marked in the 9231 pattern (FURTHER.md).
