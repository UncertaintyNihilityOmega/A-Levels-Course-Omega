## Notes for this course
- The 2028–2030 syllabus is the target (students starting now sit A Level in 2028). Exams in 2027 use the 2027
  syllabus: LEAD notes in the overview what differs. **Core studies, options and topic names change between
  syllabuses**, so take every study, author, date and finding from the syllabus and FACTS.md, never from memory.
  The option chapters are placeholders (`clinical.1` …): LEAD gives each the syllabus's topic names.
- Papers (verify): 1 approaches, issues and debates, 2 research methods (AS); 3 and 4 on the specialist options
  (A Level). Students take two options: write all four, and say so in `psych.options`.
- Each core study: aims, procedure, results, conclusions, then evaluation (methodology, ethics, reliability, validity,
  generalisability, application) in the mark-scheme language. Essay questions: model answers built band by band.
- Statistics: link the maths course where it helps (grep kit/LINKS.md for stats); numbers in examples recomputed by checks.
- Never diagnose, never give clinical advice; mental-health chapters are factual and respectful, with a short note on
  where to find help.
