:::tip How to use quizzes well
Quizzes are retrieval practice: the act of pulling an answer out of memory is what makes it stick. Do a short quiz at the start of each study session on topics from last week, not just today's topic. When you get one wrong, open the chapter it came from and redo the worked examples before trying again.
:::
